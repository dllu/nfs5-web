effort to preserve abandonware. Both state that the project is unaffiliated
with Electronic Arts and Porsche, acknowledge the respective owners of the
original content and marks, and explain that names and marks are references.

notice and GitHub link fit both sizes. Release `701408b5f9aa75b2` is deployed
at `daniel.lawrence.lu/nfs5/`, with its HTML, controls, and stylesheet verified
